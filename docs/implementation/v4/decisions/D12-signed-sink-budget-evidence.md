# D12 — Budget settlement from witnessed sink decisions

Status: bounded broker-level implementation under V4-T2-04 and prerequisite research for V4-T2-05. Specification 0.1.0 and all release thresholds remain unchanged.

## Decision

The [attested sink budget evidence helper](../../../../src/tier2/attested-sink-budget-evidence.ts) supplies `ResourceBudgetBridge.observe` and `ResourceBudgetLedger.verifySettlement` from an operator-pinned sink public anchor and branded `/2` sink state witness. It pins repository, deployment, approved adapter artifact label, ledger digest, owner, one fixed conservative charge and a bounded set of **full expected requests**. No two declarations may share the ledger's five-field effect binding. It reads the complete witnessed sink decision, checks the exact request/value/receipt and Ed25519 signature, then returns a committed observation with the fixed charge or a signed terminal noncommit fence. Missing rows, witness outage and rollback are unknown; a conflicting request under the same logical effect ID or binding is refused.

The ledger verifier independently decodes the bridge's outer settlement witness and compares the ledger, owner, **full request digest**, basic binding, disposition, value, charge and canonical inner receipt to the current sink witness head. A caller's receipt digest or `observe` claim alone cannot debit or refund the ledger. A stable policy digest covers the pinned repository, deployment, anchor, witness, adapter artifact, owner, fixed charge and complete expected-request inventory. It can be computed before ledger creation and is included in the ledger identity when historical revalidation is enabled. The full request declarations must be independently allocated and retained by the operator; this bounded profile does not itself prove which pre-split handle a direct ledger caller used. A committed fixed charge is not a measurement of actual tokens, dollars, time or memory used by an arbitrary service.

## Tested broker path

The [real-process integration test](../../../../test/tier2/attested-sink-budget-broker.test.ts) uses a separate sink and sink-witness process, the V4 signed broker, a pre-split durable budget ledger and bridge. The broker reserves before the sink call. A witnessed commit charges once; cached retry and reopened ledger/bridge do not charge again. The ledger's opt-in `revalidateSettlementOnRead` mode rechecks historical signed decisions on reopen and every read. The mode and policy digest are bound into its ledger identity; reopening with revalidation disabled, a different fixed charge or a shorter request inventory fails. A second effect receives a signed `not_committed` fence before broker reconciliation and refunds the unused reservation. An early sink-witness outage prevents broker admission before reservation. An outage after the broker's durable dispatch marker leaves a third reservation inflight; it is not refunded from absence or timeout. Once the witness restarts, signed status creates a terminal fence and recovery refunds exactly once.

The bridge's generic pre-dispatch release hook is not, by itself, evidence of noncommit. This helper refuses to verify a refund without a signed sink fence. A future versioned budgeted host/broker profile must obtain that fence or keep funds encumbered, pin the exact operator-selected budget authority outside the reloadable factory, persist deterministic grant selection, and bind its identity into broker, host and deployment histories. Existing V11 routers require `budgetReservationId: null` and cannot yet admit this bridge. The local bridge and ledger journals lack an independently held monotonic head; deleting or restoring their own signed histories together remains outside this evidence. The ledger's generic verifier callback and policy digest are selected by the process holding its signing key; the digest does not establish an independently authenticated verifier implementation if that process substitutes a permissive callback under the same digest. Opt-in historical revalidation also makes ledger reads unavailable when the sink witness is down. This checkpoint does not close T2-04, T2-05, NFR-16 or any financial performance gate.
