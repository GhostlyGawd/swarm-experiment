# Prior mechanisms for fuel-preserving semantic GC

Reviewed 2026-09-24. This is engineering comparison, not a novelty, patent-scope, or freedom-to-operate opinion. The operational shortlist is M01 (ordered virtual frame), M02 (guarded exact-source fallback), and M11 (source logical program counter). Other concepts in [review.md](review.md) remain deferred until one of these survives production integration. Links point to primary publications, a patent text, or the maker's own documentation. Search terms included `virtual frame deoptimization`, `trace relating compiler correctness`, `fuel metering optimized code`, `deterministic replay`, and `source cost semantics`.

## Concept decision after inspection

- **M01:** Proceed only as a versioned checked production profile for an exact pure forwarder. Virtual frames themselves have direct prior art. The differentiating engineering requirement here is reproducing every Aether observation under every admitted finite `maxSteps`, then carrying that result through compiled, resumable, and process paths. The 192-case [prototype](prototype-results.json) only covers the reference runtime.
- **M02:** Keep as a safety fallback when an exact event script cannot be checked. A route choice must be bound to the execution manifest before dispatch and survive crash/replay; an unrecorded guard would create a new divergence.
- **M11:** Defer as a generalization. A source-PC map is attractive but requires complete lowering and independent checking for every supported AST kind and backend, far beyond the one-wrapper evidence.

The five categories are represented for each of the three operational concepts, but this sample is small and cannot establish novelty or defensibility. Before production admission, inspect the actual parser/compiler/checkpoint/effect-ID paths and prove the exact supported wrapper grammar. Search for closer prior work again if the mechanism or product claims broaden.
